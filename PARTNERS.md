## Current Fit

Good-fit sponsor or partner categories:

- PDF API, document automation, e-signature, and file workflow software.
- QR, local marketing, print shop, and small business marketing tools.
- Resume, career, hiring, and job search tools.
- Classroom, tutoring, homeschool, and worksheet resources.
- Small business paperwork, invoicing, receipt, inventory, and operations tools.

## Pilot Options

Current low-risk pilot anchors:

- USD 49 sponsor fit review for a public product page, landing page, or partner offer.
- USD 99 to USD 149 labeled guide sponsorship pilot on a policy-fit page or directory.
- Partner distribution swap where both sides publish clear, labeled, no-payment discovery links.

The USD 49 starter review is the preferred first step because it can be reviewed manually before any sponsor copy or payment link is discussed. Pricing is a starting point for small pilots, not a guarantee of acceptance. A placement must be useful to the relevant tool audience and must not weaken the site's free-download promise.

## Safety Rules

The site does not accept or promote:

- Gambling, adult, malware, spyware, deceptive finance, crypto-heavy, or forced-ad offers.
- Fake documents, misleading upload flows, impersonation, or download-gated offers.
- Requests that require site users to submit private files, tax data, bank data, payment credentials, phone numbers, or customer records.

Do not put private payment, tax, bank, phone, customer, or identity data in GitHub issues. Use only public-safe company and product information there.

## Revenue Proof

PrintableTools Lab does not count clicks, page views, generated pages, or public issue activity as revenue. Revenue is only counted after a real qualified sponsor inquiry, signed agreement, approved platform balance, or settled external payment can be verified.
