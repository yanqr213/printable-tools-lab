After a real external provider shows paid_order_verified, generate the private customer ZIP with npm.cmd run service:delivery -- --input path/to/paid-order.json. The output stays under paid-deliverables/service-orders/, which is ignored by git.

  1. intent_received: A buyer submits the structured issue form, prefilled issue, email, or brief text. Not revenue.
  2. fit_confirmed: Confirm the request is within the simple $29 scope and details are complete enough. Not revenue.
  3. checkout_sent: Send one real external Gumroad, Payhip, Ko-fi, Stripe, or invoice checkout link. Not revenue until the provider confirms payment.
  4. paid_order_verified: Verify paid order, payout balance, or settled payment in the provider dashboard. This is the first status that can count as revenue.
  5. in_progress: Prepare the editable starter CSV, flyer copy, QR sign wording, coupon ideas, packing notes, and launch checklist. Revenue already verified externally.
  6. delivered: Send the pack through the agreed channel with review and QR-test reminders. Revenue already verified externally.
  7. revision_done: Complete the included lightweight typo or fit revision if requested. No extra revenue unless a separate new paid scope is created.
  8. closed: Log the non-sensitive outcome in OPERATIONS.md and leave private payment details in the provider only. Final counted revenue must match the external provider record.

Risk controls

  • The service does not start until a real external payment link is sent and paid.
  • No payout, tax, bank, card, identity, or platform credential is stored in the repository.
  • The buyer must review all copy, prices, QR links, and claims before printing or publishing.
  • No legal, tax, accounting, employment, medical, or financial advice is included.
  • One lightweight revision is included for typos or fit; new branding, logo design, or legal copy is out of scope.

Money gate: Revenue is proven only after a real payment provider shows a paid order, payout balance, or settled payment for this service.
