How to use this free PDF tool

  1. Open the Stamp PDF Pages and review the example text already in the form.
  2. Replace the sample fields with your own short, accurate wording.
  3. Choose US Letter or A4 before generating if the tool offers paper size options.
  4. Preview the result, then download the PDF and review it before printing, uploading, or sharing.

Privacy and limits

The source PDF is read and stamped in your browser without uploading the file to PrintableTools Lab.

The free version adds a simple status stamp to one PDF and uses the same daily generation limit as the other tools.

Frequently asked questions

Is the stamp legal proof?

No. It is a visual annotation. Keep the actual approval, payment, or audit record too.

Can I choose the stamp text?

Yes. Use a short custom label such as PAID, APPROVED, DRAFT, or REVIEW.

Are files uploaded?

No. The stamp is applied in your browser for ordinary use.
