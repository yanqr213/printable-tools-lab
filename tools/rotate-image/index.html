Rotate Image Online

Rotate or flip a JPG, PNG, or WebP image locally in your browser without uploading the file.

Select one image, rotate it 90, 180, or 270 degrees, optionally flip it, and download a corrected copy.

Rotation is a frequent helper task after phone scans, sideways photos, screenshots, and images captured in the wrong orientation.

Open generator

How to use this free image tool

  1. Open the image rotator and select one image file.
  2. Choose 90, 180, or 270 degree rotation.
  3. Optionally flip the image horizontally or vertically.
  4. Download the corrected image and confirm text and orientation look right.

Good use cases

Sideways photo

Fix a phone image that opened in the wrong orientation.

Scanned document photo

Rotate a form or receipt image before turning it into a PDF.

Mirrored image

Flip an image when a scan, selfie, or screenshot needs correction.

Privacy and limits

Selected image files are loaded into your browser and are not uploaded by the rotator.

The free version rotates one image at a time and uses the same daily generation limit as the other tools.

Frequently asked questions

Can I flip an image too?

Yes. Choose horizontal or vertical flip before export.

Are images uploaded?

No. The rotation runs in your browser.

Will it edit the original file?

No. It downloads a new rotated copy.
