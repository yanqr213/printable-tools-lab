Privacy and limits

The free version creates one static label sheet. Test scanning with your device before printing a full batch.

Frequently asked questions

What barcode type is used?

The tool draws Code 39-style bars for uppercase letters, numbers, and common symbols.

Can I use these for official retail products?

Use proper barcode registration and compliance tools for official retail distribution.

Why should codes be short?

Shorter codes print wider bars and are easier to scan on ordinary printers.
