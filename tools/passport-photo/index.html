How to use this free PDF tool

  1. Open the Passport Photo Maker and review the example text already in the form.
  2. Replace the sample fields with your own short, accurate wording.
  3. Choose US Letter or A4 before generating if the tool offers paper size options.
  4. Preview the result, then download the image file and review it before printing, uploading, or sharing.

Privacy and limits

The selected photo is cropped and rendered in your browser and is not uploaded by the maker.

The free version exports one passport-style photo or one 4x6 print sheet at a time and uses the same daily generation limit as the other tools.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The photo is loaded, cropped, and exported locally in your browser for ordinary use.

Does this guarantee official acceptance?

No. It helps with size and layout only. You still need to check official background, pose, lighting, expression, recency, and country-specific rules.

Which sizes are included?

The first version includes US 2 x 2 inch, UK 35 x 45 mm, EU-style 35 x 45 mm, Canada 50 x 70 mm, and Australia 35 x 45 mm presets.
