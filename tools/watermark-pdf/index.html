Add a light text watermark to all pages or selected PDF pages locally in your browser.

How to use this free PDF tool

  1. Open the Add Watermark to and review the example text already in the form.
  2. Replace the sample fields with your own short, accurate wording.
  3. Choose US Letter or A4 before generating if the tool offers paper size options.
  4. Preview the result, then download the PDF and review it before printing, uploading, or sharing.

Privacy and limits

The source PDF is read and watermarked in your browser without uploading the file to PrintableTools Lab.

The free version adds a text watermark to one PDF and uses the same daily generation limit as the other tools.

Yes. Enter all, a single page, or ranges such as 1,3-5.

No. The watermark is applied in your browser for ordinary use.
