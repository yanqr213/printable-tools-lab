How to use this free PDF tool

  1. Open the Price Tag Generator and review the example text already in the form.
  2. Replace the sample fields with your own short, accurate wording.
  3. Choose US Letter or A4 before generating if the tool offers paper size options.
  4. Preview the result, then download the PDF and review it before printing, uploading, or sharing.

Can I print several tags on one page?

Yes. Choose 8, 10, or 12 tags per page.

Can I use it for a craft fair?

Yes. It is designed for simple market tables, pop-up shops, and yard sales.

What should be largest?

The price should be the largest text so shoppers can scan it quickly.
