How to use this free image tool

  1. Open the image resizer and select one image file.
  2. Choose a preset or enter a custom width and optional height.
  3. Pick fit inside or fill and crop depending on whether exact dimensions matter.
  4. Download the resized image and check that important content is still visible.

Privacy and limits

Selected image files are loaded into your browser and are not uploaded by the resizer.

The free version resizes one image at a time and uses the same daily generation limit as the other tools.

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep the aspect ratio?

Yes. Use a custom width and leave height empty to keep the original ratio.

What does fill and crop do?

It fills the exact target size and trims the edges when the aspect ratios do not match.

Are images uploaded?

No. The resize runs in your browser.
