How to use this free PDF tool

  1. Open the Text to PDF Converter and review the example text already in the form.
  2. Replace the sample fields with your own short, accurate wording.
  3. Choose US Letter or A4 before generating if the tool offers paper size options.
  4. Preview the page, then download the PDF and review it before printing or sharing.

Privacy and limits

Text is rendered into the PDF in your browser. Use the AI helper only for generic wording, not private or sensitive content.

The free version is limited to one-page PDFs and a small daily generation count in the same browser while usage is validated.

Frequently asked questions

Can I paste a long document?

The first version is designed for a readable one-page PDF, so long text is trimmed in the preview.

Do I need to upload a text file?

No. Paste text into the form and generate the PDF locally.

Can I choose a font size?

Yes. Choose small, medium, or large text before generating the PDF.
