Split PDF Tool

Extract selected pages from a PDF in your browser without uploading the document.

Select one PDF, enter pages or ranges such as 1,3-5, and download a new PDF with only those pages.

PDF split searches often come from forms, applications, school packets, and admin documents where users need to remove pages quickly.

How to use this free PDF tool

  1. Open the Split PDF Tool and review the example text already in the form.
  2. Replace the sample fields with your own short, accurate wording.
  3. Choose US Letter or A4 before generating if the tool offers paper size options.
  4. Preview the page, then download the PDF and review it before printing or sharing.

Privacy and limits

The source PDF is read in your browser and is not uploaded by the split tool.

The free version extracts selected pages from one PDF and uses the same daily generation limit as the other tools.

Frequently asked questions

How do page ranges work?

Use commas and ranges such as 1,3-5. Pages are counted from the first page as 1.

Does it upload the PDF?

No. The selected PDF is processed in your browser.

Can I reorder pages?

The current tool keeps selected pages in ascending order.
