How to use this free PDF tool

  1. Open the background remover and select one JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
  2. Choose an automatic corner sample or a known background color.
  3. Adjust tolerance and edge softness until the preview looks clean.
  4. Download the transparent PNG and review it on the checkerboard preview before using it elsewhere.

Good use cases

Product or marketplace photo

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. The selected image is processed locally in your browser.

Does it use AI?

No. It uses local color matching to remove simple solid or near-solid backgrounds.

Will it work on people or hair?

Not reliably. Complex people, hair, shadows, glass, and busy backgrounds usually need a dedicated editor or manual cleanup.
