Frequently asked questions

Is this accounting software?

No. It creates a simple printable receipt PDF and does not store bookkeeping records.

Can I use it for cash payments?

Yes, choose cash or write another payment method, then keep signed copies as needed.

Does it store customer details?

No. The tool runs in the browser and does not create an account or receipt archive.
