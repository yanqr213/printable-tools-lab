Privacy and limits

Packing slips are generated locally. Avoid putting payment details or private customer notes on a package insert unless the workflow truly needs them.

The free version creates one printable packing slip PDF with a limited number of rows.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a shipping label?

No. It is a packing slip for package contents, not postage or carrier labels.

Can I use it for Etsy or marketplace orders?

Yes, for a simple package insert after you copy the order details you need.

Should prices be included?

Usually no. Packing slips often focus on items and quantities, especially for gifts.
