Frequently asked questions

Are images uploaded?

No. The selected image is processed locally in your browser.

Which format should I choose?

JPG is usually best for photos, WebP is often smaller for web use, and PNG is useful for sharp graphics.

Will it always be smaller?

Usually, but PNG output can be larger than JPG or WebP depending on the image content.
