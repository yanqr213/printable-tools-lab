How to use this free image tool

  1. Open the image to KB compressor and select one JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
  2. Choose a target such as 50KB, 100KB, 200KB, 500KB, or a custom KB value.
  3. Review the preview, estimated file size, and whether the target was met.
  4. Download the smaller image and open it before submitting it to the destination portal.

Privacy and limits

Selected image files are loaded into your browser and are not uploaded by the target-size compressor.

The free version processes one image at a time. Extremely small KB targets may require lower quality or smaller dimensions and may not be visually acceptable.

Frequently asked questions

Can it always hit 100KB?

Not always. The tool tries several quality and size combinations and shows the closest result if the target would make the image too small or blurry.

Are images uploaded?

No. Target-size compression runs locally in your browser.

Which format should I use?

JPG is a safe default for photos. WebP can be smaller when the destination site accepts it.
