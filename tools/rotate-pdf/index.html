Rotate PDF Pages

Rotate all PDF pages or selected pages locally in your browser without uploading the file.

Select one PDF, choose 90, 180, or 270 degrees, and rotate all pages or only selected page ranges.

PDF rotation is a common no-upload need for scanned forms, phone-generated packets, sideways receipts, and upside-down handouts.

Open generator

How to use this free PDF tool

  1. Open the Rotate PDF Pages and review the example text already in the form.
  2. Replace the sample fields with your own short, accurate wording.
  3. Choose US Letter or A4 before generating if the tool offers paper size options.
  4. Preview the page, then download the PDF and review it before printing or sharing.

Good use cases

Sideways scan

Fix a scanned form or receipt that was captured in the wrong orientation.

Selected page correction

Rotate only the pages that are sideways while leaving the rest untouched.

Phone-generated PDF

Correct files made from phone scans before sending or uploading them.

Privacy and limits

The PDF is read and rotated in your browser without uploading the file to PrintableTools Lab.

The free version rotates one PDF and uses the same daily generation limit as the other tools.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate only one page?

Yes. Enter that page number or a range such as 2 or 2-4.

Does this upload the PDF?

No. The rotation runs in your browser for ordinary use.

Does it edit the original file?

No. It downloads a new rotated copy.
