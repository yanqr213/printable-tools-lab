Why this search has urgency

PDF-to-Word searches often happen when someone must edit a report, letter, assignment, resume, or form right before submitting it. Many converters require upload, queueing, signup, or a paid export step.

What this free version does

The browser reads selectable PDF text and writes a clean DOCX with page headings. It is useful for editing the words, not for perfect visual layout reconstruction.

Important limit

Scanned image-only PDFs need OCR first, and complex tables, columns, forms, or legal formatting may be simplified. Keep the original PDF when exact layout matters.
