Manual execution checklist

  1. Start with 5 to 10 manual, relevant, non-spam contacts where the service solves an immediate print or market-table problem.
  2. Send the request brief first; do not ask for payment until the buyer confirms fit and details.
  3. Use the external checkout provider only after the buyer asks to proceed.
  4. Log any request URL, reply, or paid order in OPERATIONS.md with the date and source.
  5. Count revenue only from a paid provider order, payout balance, or settled payment.

Risk controls

  • Do not spam communities or scrape private contact lists.
  • Do not promise guaranteed sales, legal compliance, tax results, or ad performance.
  • Do not collect card, bank, payout, tax, or identity details in the repository or GitHub issues.
  • Do not begin paid custom work without a real external payment record.

Money gate: Revenue is proven only after a real payment provider shows a paid order, payout balance, or settled payment for this service.
