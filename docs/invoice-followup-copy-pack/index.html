Invoice Follow-up Copy Pack for freelancers and small teams

A tiny done-for-you service for people who just made an invoice and need professional follow-up words without asking for legal, tax, collection, or accounting advice. The buyer sends public-safe context, preferred tone, due date, and invoice status; the seller returns editable reminder copy they can review and send themselves.

After paid_order_verified, run npm.cmd run service:delivery -- --input path/to/paid-order.json. Private customer ZIPs stay under paid-deliverables/service-orders/.

  1. intent_received: Not revenue.
  2. fit_confirmed: Not revenue.
  3. checkout_sent: Not revenue until the provider confirms payment.
  4. paid_order_verified: This is the first status that can count as revenue.
  5. in_progress: Revenue already verified externally.
  6. delivered: Revenue already verified externally.
  7. revision_done: No extra revenue unless a separate new paid scope is created.
  8. closed: Final counted revenue must match the external provider record.

Risk controls

  • The service provides editable communication copy only, not legal, tax, accounting, debt-collection, or financial advice.
  • Do not send invoice numbers, customer private data, bank details, card data, tax IDs, identity documents, or full client lists.
  • The buyer must review every message for accuracy, tone, jurisdiction, and client relationship before sending.
  • The service does not contact the buyer's client, collect payments, or guarantee payment outcomes.
  • Work starts only after a real external checkout or invoice is paid and verified.

Money gate: Revenue is proven only after a real payment provider shows a paid order, payout balance, or settled payment for this invoice follow-up service.
