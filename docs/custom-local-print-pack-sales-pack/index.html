Private delivery command after paid_order_verified: npm.cmd run service:delivery -- --input path/to/paid-order.json. Public sample files show structure only.

  1. intent_received: Not revenue.
  2. fit_confirmed: Not revenue.
  3. checkout_sent: Not revenue until the provider confirms payment.
  4. paid_order_verified: This is the first status that can count as revenue.
  5. in_progress: Revenue already verified externally.
  6. delivered: Revenue already verified externally.
  7. revision_done: No extra revenue unless a separate new paid scope is created.
  8. closed: Final counted revenue must match the external provider record.

Manual execution checklist

  1. Start with 5 to 10 manual, relevant, non-spam contacts where the service solves an immediate print or market-table problem.
  2. Send the request brief first; do not ask for payment until the buyer confirms fit and details.
  3. Use the external checkout provider only after the buyer asks to proceed.
  4. After paid_order_verified, run npm.cmd run service:delivery -- --input path/to/order.json to generate the private delivery ZIP under paid-deliverables/service-orders.
  5. Log any request URL, reply, or paid order in OPERATIONS.md with the date and source.
  6. Count revenue only from a paid provider order, payout balance, or settled payment.

Risk controls

  • Do not spam communities or scrape private contact lists.
  • Do not promise guaranteed sales, legal compliance, tax results, or ad performance.
  • Do not collect card, bank, payout, tax, or identity details in the repository or GitHub issues.
  • Do not begin paid custom work without a real external payment record.
