The buyer sends product, service, or event details; the seller returns editable starter content that can be pasted into the PrintableTools Lab generators and printed.

After paid_order_verified, run npm.cmd run service:delivery -- --input path/to/paid-order.json. Private customer ZIPs stay under paid-deliverables/service-orders/.

  1. intent_received: Not revenue.
  2. fit_confirmed: Not revenue.
  3. checkout_sent: Not revenue until the provider confirms payment.
  4. paid_order_verified: This is the first status that can count as revenue.
  5. in_progress: Revenue already verified externally.
  6. delivered: Revenue already verified externally.
  7. revision_done: No extra revenue unless a separate new paid scope is created.
  8. closed: Final counted revenue must match the external provider record.

Risk controls

  • The service does not start until a real external payment link is sent and paid.
  • No payout, tax, bank, card, identity, or platform credential is stored in the repository.
  • The buyer must review all copy, prices, QR links, and claims before printing or publishing.
  • No legal, tax, accounting, employment, medical, or financial advice is included.
  • One lightweight revision is included for typos or fit; new branding, logo design, or legal copy is out of scope.

Money gate: Revenue is proven only after a real payment provider shows a paid order, payout balance, or settled payment for this service.
