Pick the target from the error message

Use the exact KB number shown by the portal whenever possible. If the site says under 100KB, use the 100KB target; if it says under 20KB or 50KB, expect stronger quality tradeoffs.

Local target-size workflow

The browser tries different dimensions and compression levels locally, then exports the closest smaller JPG or WebP it can make. The original image is not uploaded during ordinary use.

When to resize first

If the portal also gives pixel dimensions, resize or crop before compressing. This is especially important for passport-style, exam, school, and profile photos.
