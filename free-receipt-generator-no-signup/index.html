Invoice vs receipt

Use an invoice before payment is due. Use a receipt after payment is made and both sides need a record.

What to include

Payer, recipient, payment date, amount, method, description, and a short note. Keep copies with your own records.

Limits

This is a practical receipt format, not legal, tax, or accounting advice. Requirements vary by business type and location.
