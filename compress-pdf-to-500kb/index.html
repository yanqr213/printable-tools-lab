Why this is high intent

A 500KB PDF limit usually appears after a user has already tried to submit a form, school file, job document, exam upload, or government document and been blocked.

Local target-size workflow

The browser renders selected pages into smaller JPEG-backed PDF pages and tries stronger compression passes when a target size is selected.

Honest limit

A 500KB target can be too small for long or text-heavy PDFs. The tool tries to get close, but the result may flatten selectable text and can lose detail.
