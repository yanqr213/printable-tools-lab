Placement rules

  • Downloads stay free and cannot require an ad click, sponsor interaction, account, or payment.
  • Sponsor copy must be clearly labeled and separated from generator controls.
  • No gambling, adult, deceptive finance, malware, fake document, or misleading upload-service offers.
  • No claim of guaranteed traffic, guaranteed compression, legal/tax advice, or official government acceptance.
  • Payment, tax, bank, phone, and identity details stay in external official provider dashboards only.
