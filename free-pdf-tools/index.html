Why the tools are free

The validation version is free because the project is testing which document and image jobs attract real search traffic and repeat downloads. If ads are enabled later, they should sit away from generator controls and never become a condition for downloading.

For privacy-sensitive jobs, avoid entering unnecessary personal details. Image and PDF processing stays local in the browser; optional AI suggestions are limited to generic writing fields.
